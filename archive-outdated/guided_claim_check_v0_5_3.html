Question 1 · scope test

What family does this passage belong to?

Decide by what the passage is about, not by whether it feels important.

Question 1a · always-on or switched-on

Always-on, or switched-on?

Ask what the document says, not why the measure exists. Always-on means it applies to every model all the time. Switched-on means the text ties it to a stated line, e.g. "for High capability models". The line need not name a hazard — a capability level counts.

Question 1b · consequence citation

Does it cite C, B, R, or N content as a downstream consequence?

For example, an AI R&D threshold citing accelerated CBRN weapons development.

Question 2 · is there a fact of the matter

Is there something here that either happened or didn’t?

Not "is it true" and not "could it be argued" — is there a fact of the matter. It names an action, condition, threshold, actor, process, or trigger. Mission statements and undefined abstractions do not. This is about the whole passage; whether a condition is specific enough to be a threshold is Rule 3, and only comes up if you route to that group.

Question 3 · restatement

Have you already coded this assertion in this document?

Core gates often restate three to five times. Same assertion with no field value changing means no new record.

Question 4 · field mapping

Which conditional groups can you populate?

Select every group the passage supports. Most claims support one, some support two. If you select none, this is a field-gap log entry.

There are nine in-scope families but eight groups. Framework versioning and revision has no group of its own: a commitment to update or revise the framework codes under Decision point as framework_revision. The five version.* fields are document-level and are never populated from a claim.

Two or more groups selected. If the fields diverge on claim type, hazard, decision point, or backing, this is a split — code two records.

Question 5 · document fact check

Is the only content a read-off document property?

A named multilateral commitment, a version property, or an evaluator access term — something no claim in the document asserts. Publication commitments are NOT in this category: "we will publish summaries of these assessments" is a Measure 10.2 commitment and belongs in the evaluation group above.

Answer the questions above first.
